Nether portals must be made out of the elusive Komatiite, which can not be mined.
Electron tubes need silver, not iron.
Pools of gravity-defying levitite can rarely be found in islands high above.
Many biomes are around the islands. The undersides may have cave biomes too.
Trains are quite fast and can be used for cross-dimension transport.
Trial chambers can be found floating, filled with loot for those strong enough.
Deep caverns exist in the thin layer below the ocean. Rare minerals can be found within.
Nether portals must be at least 15 blocks big. A 3x5 is the most efficent.
There are hidden veins of ore-bearing rock only accessable with drillling machines.
Andesite can be found in rare large rock stripes or in some surface rocks.
Clay is found in islands of all heights.
Rarely you can find massive pillars from the ocean floor to near the top of the world.
Mineshafts commonly spawn below the ocean floor.
Backpack upgrades can only be found from trial chambers.
Big islands may contain large flat areas.
The ocean floor can contain large amounts of prismarine, sand, gravel, and basalt.
Pirate ships rarely dot the seas.
