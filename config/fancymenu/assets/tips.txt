Nether portals must be made out of the elusive Komatiite, which can not be mined.
Electron tubes need silver, not iron.
Pools of gravity-defying levitite can rarely be found in islands high above.
Many biomes are around the islands. The undersides may have cave biomes too.
Trains are quite fast and can be used for cross-dimension transport.
Trial chambers can be found floating, filled with loot for those strong enough.
Deep caverns exist in the thin layer below the ocean. Rare minerals can be found within.
